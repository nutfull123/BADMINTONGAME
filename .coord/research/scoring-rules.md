# Official BWF Scoring System Research

## Summary
- The BWF membership officially approved the **3×15 scoring system** (best of 3 games to 15 points, rally-point scoring, hard cap at 21) at the 87th BWF Annual General Meeting in Horsens, Denmark on **April 25, 2026** ([bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHimWWkfep1nxmZh2xQlB0zmItLRpPMt29tDMpA27kzZflayp0wC6IqpwPb7eF2eJZLn_Oz6fmspxyUVLfrFTIrwITmx8PrVQzsb3JkKg5KXDL5yf4NYSlDcYQ_nYkcmlL2Z0kYWIt0bCB5uXPw0rVgB0aVmIkgpDTGjHVi0Zg79c4VoCUuv_yWj6Xxl3g=)).
- While **3×15 is officially adopted into the Laws of Badminton** (no longer merely a proposal or trial), its effective rollout date across international BWF competitions is **January 4, 2027** (Week 1 of the 2027 calendar); until that date, standard 2026 BWF World Tour events continue under the **3×21 system** (best of 3 games to 21 points, cap at 30) aside from designated 3×15 trial events ([bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGL1Uh2dQpv2tcNhJDGqCs849Jaa91d91mY3klFHalEHlAi-SnBPLFPqx1YGYSVA5wbd5Em5CekxCqkRAWkXVKvWdq70tKZSpDdN1uaifA5j7DebhRxzqWqekHAVrSK2gNaRMJpqWF8RNSBM7UzKTNTUoO-lXGnppctRPt4SOHn6mBo0FsGaI9-KAUkKq1o)).
- All core rally-point and singles service mechanics (even score right court, odd score left court, rally winner scores a point and serves next) remain identical between the 3×15 and 3×21 systems, with only the target score (15 vs 21), deuce trigger (14-all vs 20-all), hard cap (21 vs 30), and mid-game interval/end-change threshold (8 points vs 11 points) changing ([bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHtvPMrpvqnbktc-pFKaox1TzJflI2Vkb6HOSNDM_TaJXnS4AVOlH_fRDUR6GITWAPOQG2snBUAq6hLPNBMlYfECGiDWOTFdsh1maTJ0b0t3FGpOpnm7LuOZIdpnnG3KbaAyi4M3we6LBsrCO7Z1ALHU5lVJ3Gxrv9Z5hiD95BS8pncAN87YMUqRsR5CxmDag==), [corporate.bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHok2axlyZSMNbQ9AL9Dym9iu8LVTO1BRTBXvjv1etp0VI_pGmVdTD_I9NaCfQ7h5562CCE-OSKSocMUf2Cm1weA2pOQ1-o0GffwvqaLmheOetINv_7fM5t2jMs5MxCXsJd-UG_cBVsTvWMg3Cu_j-dk6vK1GxLRv2HdRboTqwZegoF3t0E1TBXeZ_3FJKeVgFTuTYPsHmJhghhYmnEsY-AmujnpskbaX3Zyhk8b8N5Zjl0NRgzIhHWVQaRgBTB)).

## Rules

1. **Points per game & games per match**
   - **Adopted 3×15 System (effective Jan 4, 2027):** A match consists of the **best of 3 games** (first side to win 2 games wins the match). Each game is played to **15 points** using **rally-point scoring** (a point is awarded on every rally regardless of which side served) ([bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEONR0Xpary6pnCSo3eUPmrnn0ZEtRp7qIp90u6hl1B54aTdQzl05Xgh-huL8OQjJrNwb9Ol16tTy3HBl9-oJzCANaBvWJ2vtZu4UOQ3iuwMNoRxio5sADqKLOhVAPsy1AyisngQSP_11EuNzehZylB0n668PuUJNkMEMKM2TlOKGJ61Jywowv9TN3J7k0=), [bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGL1Uh2dQpv2tcNhJDGqCs849Jaa91d91mY3klFHalEHlAi-SnBPLFPqx1YGYSVA5wbd5Em5CekxCqkRAWkXVKvWdq70tKZSpDdN1uaifA5j7DebhRxzqWqekHAVrSK2gNaRMJpqWF8RNSBM7UzKTNTUoO-lXGnppctRPt4SOHn6mBo0FsGaI9-KAUkKq1o)).
   - **Outgoing 3×21 System (Law 7, retained as an approved alternate):** Best of 3 games to **21 points** with rally-point scoring ([corporate.bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHHQ_FFQzmSgq2wLK1M59hJsRbr42AjLg4cbOYj_m535dfgjxVoluDDHVhn5kO4JGQgB6Rt0nwmLp_ni0BPniInJmsNJCg0wp1nZENelLmVV2cofdynjWckJZR0aoco1JUGqTuHY7bD7_mYb1tgb-lR-jMgdcQYI0HLbXfGERnd43_NtX7vzL_Rqnu4iDNJx98MlBRK5M850fZchNFGIKKKGK_5mWpdL0XIvQ__PflILBtHy20PSacbc1xg1HKa1Iop), [bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHtvPMrpvqnbktc-pFKaox1TzJflI2Vkb6HOSNDM_TaJXnS4AVOlH_fRDUR6GITWAPOQG2snBUAq6hLPNBMlYfECGiDWOTFdsh1maTJ0b0t3FGpOpnm7LuOZIdpnnG3KbaAyi4M3we6LBsrCO7Z1ALHU5lVJ3Gxrv9Z5hiD95BS8pncAN87YMUqRsR5CxmDag==)).

2. **Deuce / win-by-2 rule and hard cap score**
   - **Adopted 3×15 System:** If the score reaches **14–all**, the game is extended and won by the first side to gain a **2-point lead** (e.g., 16–14, 17–15). The **hard cap is 21 points**: if the score reaches **20–all**, the first side to score the **21st point** wins the game (21–20 maximum score) ([bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEONR0Xpary6pnCSo3eUPmrnn0ZEtRp7qIp90u6hl1B54aTdQzl05Xgh-huL8OQjJrNwb9Ol16tTy3HBl9-oJzCANaBvWJ2vtZu4UOQ3iuwMNoRxio5sADqKLOhVAPsy1AyisngQSP_11EuNzehZylB0n668PuUJNkMEMKM2TlOKGJ61Jywowv9TN3J7k0=), [bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGL1Uh2dQpv2tcNhJDGqCs849Jaa91d91mY3klFHalEHlAi-SnBPLFPqx1YGYSVA5wbd5Em5CekxCqkRAWkXVKvWdq70tKZSpDdN1uaifA5j7DebhRxzqWqekHAVrSK2gNaRMJpqWF8RNSBM7UzKTNTUoO-lXGnppctRPt4SOHn6mBo0FsGaI9-KAUkKq1o)).
   - **Outgoing 3×21 System:** At **20–all**, the side that gains a 2-point lead first wins that game; at **29–all**, the side scoring the **30th point** wins that game ([corporate.bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHHQ_FFQzmSgq2wLK1M59hJsRbr42AjLg4cbOYj_m535dfgjxVoluDDHVhn5kO4JGQgB6Rt0nwmLp_ni0BPniInJmsNJCg0wp1nZENelLmVV2cofdynjWckJZR0aoco1JUGqTuHY7bD7_mYb1tgb-lR-jMgdcQYI0HLbXfGERnd43_NtX7vzL_Rqnu4iDNJx98MlBRK5M850fZchNFGIKKKGK_5mWpdL0XIvQ__PflILBtHy20PSacbc1xg1HKa1Iop)).

3. **Interval rule and change of ends**
   - **Mid-game interval (Law 16.2.1):** An interval not exceeding **60 seconds** occurs during each game when the leading score first reaches **8 points** in the 3×15 system (changed from **11 points** in the 3×21 system) ([bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGL1Uh2dQpv2tcNhJDGqCs849Jaa91d91mY3klFHalEHlAi-SnBPLFPqx1YGYSVA5wbd5Em5CekxCqkRAWkXVKvWdq70tKZSpDdN1uaifA5j7DebhRxzqWqekHAVrSK2gNaRMJpqWF8RNSBM7UzKTNTUoO-lXGnppctRPt4SOHn6mBo0FsGaI9-KAUkKq1o), [corporate.bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEF2wqlpqvSfL57flXFrSAnZ6zcTlIjJ9TBUQk-JRt1j1TPmzApI-OxJyND_3LjdUAiWQJT_7uDFx4o_92dy1bmGYriEcLtQqBZGqpxiDDIHsJLh2J0YGR1GUfshk11sjdBpUQr4qkxYbUfw-z7js2HVwCpn_9Mim0mi4KXFe_ACbfa6K64bTf-R2GvTArPtemH_VoqCMMAJonRLCBXrZG0505QzcERSyvFG7EVYXHu4K0zoPFyA7rVuQuwm1r-)).
   - **Between-game intervals (Law 16.2.2):** An interval not exceeding **120 seconds (2 minutes)** is allowed between the first and second game, and between the second and third game (unchanged from 3×21) ([bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGL1Uh2dQpv2tcNhJDGqCs849Jaa91d91mY3klFHalEHlAi-SnBPLFPqx1YGYSVA5wbd5Em5CekxCqkRAWkXVKvWdq70tKZSpDdN1uaifA5j7DebhRxzqWqekHAVrSK2gNaRMJpqWF8RNSBM7UzKTNTUoO-lXGnppctRPt4SOHn6mBo0FsGaI9-KAUkKq1o), [corporate.bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEF2wqlpqvSfL57flXFrSAnZ6zcTlIjJ9TBUQk-JRt1j1TPmzApI-OxJyND_3LjdUAiWQJT_7uDFx4o_92dy1bmGYriEcLtQqBZGqpxiDDIHsJLh2J0YGR1GUfshk11sjdBpUQr4qkxYbUfw-z7js2HVwCpn_9Mim0mi4KXFe_ACbfa6K64bTf-R2GvTArPtemH_VoqCMMAJonRLCBXrZG0505QzcERSyvFG7EVYXHu4K0zoPFyA7rVuQuwm1r-)).
   - **Change of ends (Law 8):** Players change ends: (a) at the end of the first game; (b) at the end of the second game, if there is to be a third game; and (c) in the third game, when a side first reaches **8 points** in the 3×15 system (changed from **11 points** in the 3×21 system) ([bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEONR0Xpary6pnCSo3eUPmrnn0ZEtRp7qIp90u6hl1B54aTdQzl05Xgh-huL8OQjJrNwb9Ol16tTy3HBl9-oJzCANaBvWJ2vtZu4UOQ3iuwMNoRxio5sADqKLOhVAPsy1AyisngQSP_11EuNzehZylB0n668PuUJNkMEMKM2TlOKGJ61Jywowv9TN3J7k0=), [corporate.bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEtTQMJBAodyk9VvVQgagJa8YDTIwOYgqIAbiQJn5emzOr8UdzXrfMTWuuMRoD4wGwrnDae1i5uxZ4PVTLSedXvDjGI3HaDp5XpZd_fkOPeEfDVK9PpIvOPV948FJeFCGOX7kEzAM-4NHP7wUrC1k6EFIN4ZKr37puV_u-W2S9mcQOCFXZaksL_oGcoAmu2DKBoJkSgyLA37UbOKS2ReanNO3_oo-IXpxUvXRBMndgrmCEmPdQXhzJiiW0cZ-Q=)).

4. **Singles service rules (Law 7.3, Law 9, Law 10)**
   - **No change vs. the 21-point system:** All service court and service rotation rules in singles remain identical under 3×15 ([bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHtvPMrpvqnbktc-pFKaox1TzJflI2Vkb6HOSNDM_TaJXnS4AVOlH_fRDUR6GITWAPOQG2snBUAq6hLPNBMlYfECGiDWOTFdsh1maTJ0b0t3FGpOpnm7LuOZIdpnnG3KbaAyi4M3we6LBsrCO7Z1ALHU5lVJ3Gxrv9Z5hiD95BS8pncAN87YMUqRsR5CxmDag==), [corporate.bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHok2axlyZSMNbQ9AL9Dym9iu8LVTO1BRTBXvjv1etp0VI_pGmVdTD_I9NaCfQ7h5562CCE-OSKSocMUf2Cm1weA2pOQ1-o0GffwvqaLmheOetINv_7fM5t2jMs5MxCXsJd-UG_cBVsTvWMg3Cu_j-dk6vK1GxLRv2HdRboTqwZegoF3t0E1TBXeZ_3FJKeVgFTuTYPsHmJhghhYmnEsY-AmujnpskbaX3Zyhk8b8N5Zjl0NRgzIhHWVQaRgBTB)).
   - **Court side by score (Law 10.1):** The server and receiver stand in their respective **right service courts** when the server has scored **0 or an even number** of points in that game, and in their respective **left service courts** when the server has scored an **odd number** of points in that game ([corporate.bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHok2axlyZSMNbQ9AL9Dym9iu8LVTO1BRTBXvjv1etp0VI_pGmVdTD_I9NaCfQ7h5562CCE-OSKSocMUf2Cm1weA2pOQ1-o0GffwvqaLmheOetINv_7fM5t2jMs5MxCXsJd-UG_cBVsTvWMg3Cu_j-dk6vK1GxLRv2HdRboTqwZegoF3t0E1TBXeZ_3FJKeVgFTuTYPsHmJhghhYmnEsY-AmujnpskbaX3Zyhk8b8N5Zjl0NRgzIhHWVQaRgBTB)).
   - **Rally outcome (Law 10.2):** If the server wins a rally, the server scores a point and serves again from the alternate service court. If the receiver wins a rally, the receiver scores a point, becomes the new server, and serves from the appropriate service court (right if their new score is even, left if odd) ([corporate.bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHok2axlyZSMNbQ9AL9Dym9iu8LVTO1BRTBXvjv1etp0VI_pGmVdTD_I9NaCfQ7h5562CCE-OSKSocMUf2Cm1weA2pOQ1-o0GffwvqaLmheOetINv_7fM5t2jMs5MxCXsJd-UG_cBVsTvWMg3Cu_j-dk6vK1GxLRv2HdRboTqwZegoF3t0E1TBXeZ_3FJKeVgFTuTYPsHmJhghhYmnEsY-AmujnpskbaX3Zyhk8b8N5Zjl0NRgzIhHWVQaRgBTB)).
   - **Next game server (Law 7.5):** The side winning a game serves first in the next game ([bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHNO-JprtCxkuJ-FJCU9XEEqwpcwMKX94fqJflsQO6tmm8YwRYrbE5oJ8Uovg1058BuiJIE-FJpLbAHGhpX0-n-K9B5x9ukO5JZQAwvJRyXiD1XW59zgHdWO5CuQlOSXjI2WJYvfobkScOyp2g785fC0m-33n79_-bBlYzASPcVOcnU56vB5KnZI6oVjrA9zQ==)).
   - **Service delivery (Law 9.1.6):** Fixed-height service rule applies—the whole shuttle must be below **1.15 metres** from the court surface at the instant of being hit by the server's racket ([corporate.bwfbadminton.com](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEtTQMJBAodyk9VvVQgagJa8YDTIwOYgqIAbiQJn5emzOr8UdzXrfMTWuuMRoD4wGwrnDae1i5uxZ4PVTLSedXvDjGI3HaDp5XpZd_fkOPeEfDVK9PpIvOPV948FJeFCGOX7kEzAM-4NHP7wUrC1k6EFIN4ZKr37puV_u-W2S9mcQOCFXZaksL_oGcoAmu2DKBoJkSgyLA37UbOKS2ReanNO3_oo-IXpxUvXRBMndgrmCEmPdQXhzJiiW0cZ-Q=)).
