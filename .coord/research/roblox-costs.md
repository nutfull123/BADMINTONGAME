## Hidden or Later Costs

- **All-ages publishing deposit (2026):** While Studio and hosting are free, opening a new experience to under-16 audiences (*Roblox Kids* / *Roblox Select*) now requires either **1,000 Robux** (~$10 USD on web, refundable after 90 days), 2 months of Roblox Plus/Premium, or organic engagement first.
- **Effective revenue take-home vs. player spend:** On a 1,000 Robux game pass (~$10 USD paid by a web player), Roblox takes 30% (leaving 700 Robux), and cashing out at **$0.0038/Robux** yields **$2.66 USD** (~26.6% of player web spend, before Tipalti payout/FX fees and local taxes).
- **Group ownership & video assets:** Creating a Group costs **100 Robux** (~$1), and uploading any `VideoFrame` asset costs **2,000 Robux** (~$20, non-refundable even if moderation rejects it).
- **Cross-border payout friction (Thailand):** Reaching the **30,000 Earned Robux** ($114 USD) DevEx minimum, completing W-8BEN tax forms, and paying Tipalti transfer/currency-conversion fees all apply before cash reaches a Thai bank account.
- **Discovery / Marketing:** Publishing organically is free, but getting initial players on Roblox often leads creators to spend optional budget in Ads Manager ($5+ initial card billing or irreversible Ad Credits).

## Bottom Line

Building, hosting multiplayer servers (including free reserved servers for playing with friends), and storing player data in DataStores on Roblox cost **$0/month** regardless of how many players join, eliminating the Fly.io server bill. The only real upfront costs are optional or small one-time items—**100 Robux** (~$1) for a Group and a **refundable 1,000 Robux** (~$10) deposit if you want immediate all-ages (*Kids*/*Select*) publishing without waiting for organic engagement—while images, meshes, and up to 100+ audio files/month are free. If you later monetize, Roblox's effective cut is steep (~26.6% net USD to the creator after the 30% fee and $0.0038/Robux DevEx rate, with a 30,000 Robux minimum cash-out), though creators in Thailand can cash out via Tipalti with a W-8BEN form.

## Lead check against the official pages (2026-11-23, read directly)

- Publishing (create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-experiences-and-places): private and 16+/Trusted Friends releases have no fee (account in good standing and at least 2 days old, age check by facial estimation or government ID, content questionnaire). All ages (including Roblox Kids and Select) needs 2FA, the full questionnaire, the Kids/Select evaluation, and either "a one-time, refundable fee of 1,000 Robux per game" or an active Roblox Plus or Premium subscription for 2 consecutive months. Correction: the fee is refunded "If your game maintains 25 highly engaged players for 60 days without moderation" (the 90-day condition belongs to the optional 50,000 Robux expedited review). Up to 5 new games can be released per day.
- Data stores (create.roblox.com/docs/cloud-services/data-stores/error-codes-and-limits): "Total latest version storage limit = 500 MB + 1 MB × lifetime user count"; going over limits throttles or rejects requests; no charge is mentioned.
- DevEx (create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange): minimum 30,000 Earned Robux; standard rate 0.0038 USD per Earned Robux ($114 for 30,000); 0.0054 for certain purchases by U.S. players verified 18+; 0.0035 for Robux earned before September 5, 2025; one completed request per calendar month.
