## Risks & Technical Mitigations

1. **Mixamo & Bandai Namco License Traps:**
   - **Risk:** Developers frequently drop Mixamo `.glb` files or Bandai Namco `.bvh` files into a web game's `public/` directory. For a browser game, `public/*.glb` files are served as raw downloadable files, which violates Adobe's prohibition on redistributing raw Mixamo assets and Bandai Namco's Non-Commercial (`CC BY-NC 4.0`) clause.
   - **Mitigation:** Stick strictly to **Quaternius (CC0)**, **MakeHuman/MPFB2 core (CC0)**, **Poly Haven (CC0)**, and **ambientCG (CC0)**.
2. **Canned Animation vs. Physics-Driven `racketHead` Mismatch:**
   - **Risk:** A canned forehand/backhand animation from any library swings the hand along a fixed curve, whereas our gameplay (`src/sim/index.ts`) requires the racket head to sit at `player.racketHead` (controlled by mouse or `autoRacket`).
   - **Mitigation:** Use a **hybrid animation + IK approach**: drive the lower body, torso, head, and off-arm from the glTF `AnimationMixer` (chosen by `player.action` and `footworkOf(player)`), then apply our `solveTwoBoneIK` in `onBeforeRender` / post-mixer update to aim the right upper arm, forearm, and hand at `player.racketHead`.
3. **Character Height Scaling (`tall 1.95 m`, `balanced 1.78 m`, `short 1.63 m`):**
   - **Risk:** Uniformly scaling a glTF character mesh changes limb lengths and foot contact height if the rig origin is not at the feet (`y = 0`).
   - **Mitigation:** Export/normalize the character `.glb` with its root pivot at `y = 0` (floor level between the feet) and reference height `1.78 m`, then scale the root group by `BODY_HEIGHT[body] / 1.78` and compute shoulder world position before solving the right-arm IK to `player.racketHead`.
4. **Texture & HDRI Bandwidth Bloat:**
   - **Risk:** Poly Haven and ambientCG default to 4K/8K PNGs and 4K EXRs (20–80 MB each), which will stall a browser game's startup.
   - **Mitigation:** Only ship **1K JPG/WebP** texture maps (or Basis/KTX2 compressed textures) and a **1K `.hdr`** (or pre-baked PMREM / `RoomEnvironment`), keeping total asset payload under 4 MB.
