## 1. Quaternius Male & Female Humanoid Characters (Shared Rig Comparison & Poly Pizza Direct GLBs)

### Evaluation: *Universal Base Characters* vs. *Ultimate Modular Men & Women* vs. *Poly Pizza Single GLBs*

1. **Universal Base Characters (`quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html` / `quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters`)**:
   - **Why it is the newest native match for UAL 1 & 2:** Built on the exact same 65-bone UE5-compatible Universal Humanoid Rig as *Universal Animation Library 1 & 2* (`pelvis`, `spine_01`, `upperarm_r`, `lowerarm_r`, `hand_r`, `thigh_l`, `calf_l`, etc.). Includes 6 base meshes (Male and Female in *Regular*, *Superhero*, and *Teen* proportions, ~13k triangles) plus 20 mix-and-match hairstyles in `.FBX` and `.glTF`/`.glb`.
   - **Download caveat:** Hosted on itch.io (`Universal Base Characters[Standard].zip`, ~122 MB [uncertain]); requires an interactive browser click on itch.io's download button (no unauthenticated static CDN URL) and extracting two base character `.glb` meshes from the zip.
2. **Ultimate Modular Men & Ultimate Modular Women (`quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html` & `ultimatemodularwomen.html`)**:
   - **Why it works:** Lower-poly stylized athletic/casual characters (~3,280–4,318 triangles) with 24 built-in humanoid animations already embedded inside each `.glb` file, plus a humanoid rig compatible with retargeting from *Universal Animation Library*.
   - **Full-pack download caveat:** The full pack buttons on `quaternius.com` open Google Drive folders (`drive.google.com/drive/folders/...`), which require a browser click to download the folder archive.
3. **Poly Pizza Individual GLB Hosting (`poly.pizza` / `static.poly.pizza`)**:
   - **Why it is the cleanest direct-download option:** Poly Pizza hosts the individual rigged and animated `.glb` characters from Quaternius's *Ultimate Modular Men* and *Ultimate Modular Women* packs as standalone CC0 1.0 `.glb` files on `https://static.poly.pizza/<ResourceID>.glb` (~1.36–1.46 MB each, verified via `HTTP/2 200` `HEAD` requests), each containing the mesh, skeleton, and 24 embedded animation clips.

> **Important License Finding on `quaternius.com/license.html`:** While every individual pack page on `quaternius.com` (`universalbasecharacters.html`, `ultimatemodularcharacters.html`, `ultimatemodularwomen.html`, `universalanimationlibrary.html`, `universalanimationlibrary2.html`) explicitly displays `License: CC0` linking to `https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/` and states *"Free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects. (CC0 License)"*, the top navbar's `https://quaternius.com/license.html` page also publishes the *Quaternius Asset License (QAL) v1.0* (`"You can use these assets, free of charge, in personal, educational, and commercial games and other projects, with no credit required. You just can't resell or redistribute the assets themselves as assets."` and *"It does not restrict distributing a completed Product that merely incorporates the Assets"*). Both the CC0 1.0 declaration on the pack pages and QAL v1.0 Section 2 explicitly allow shipping the assets inside a public/commercial game without attribution.
